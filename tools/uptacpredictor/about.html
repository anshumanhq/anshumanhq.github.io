Our Core Values

The principles that shape every decision behind this project.

Transparency

All predictions are clearly marked as estimates, based on real historical data — never guesswork dressed up as certainty.

Accessibility

No signup, no paywalls. Every UPTAC aspirant deserves access to good information, regardless of background.

Simplicity

Built to be usable in seconds, even by someone checking results on a phone during a stressful counselling week.

The Journey

How It Started

A small idea that grew out of a real problem faced during UPTAC counselling.

The Problem

Scattered PDFs and confusing cutoff lists made it hard for students to plan their choice-filling strategy.

The Build

Three years of official cutoff data were organized into one clean, searchable, predictive tool.

The Launch

UPTACPredict went live — free for every aspirant, with no strings attached.
